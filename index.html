04

Questions

What's the difference between the packages?

Each one builds on the one below it. Basic is outside glass only. Bronze is glass inside and outside. Silver adds frames and sills. Gold adds screens and window tracks. Platinum adds scraping paint and overspray off the glass and a deionized water rinse.

Do I need to be home?

Only if we're cleaning inside. Basic is outside only, so nobody needs to be home. Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum all include inside glass, so someone needs to be there to let us in.

How much does it cost?

We price by the window, so it depends on your home. Tap "Get your quote now," tell us where you are and roughly how many windows, and we'll get back to you with a price.

How far ahead can I book?

The schedule above always shows exactly which days are open, about four weeks at a time, Monday through Saturday. Online booking is closed on major holidays. Need something sooner than the first open day? Call or text 517-759-2607.

Will I get a confirmation?

Yes. You'll get an email the moment you book, and a reminder email the day before your visit.

How do I change or cancel?

Call or text Declan at 517-759-2607, or just reply to your confirmation email.

What is deionized water?

Water with the minerals taken out. Regular tap water leaves spots when it dries; deionized water doesn't. It's the final rinse in our Platinum package.
